EtherTrack — Carbon Credit Trading & CCTS Compliance Platform for India

EtherTrack is India's blockchain-native carbon infrastructure system. Track GHG emissions across Scope 1, 2 and 3, tokenise verified carbon credits on Ethereum, trade on a live marketplace, retire credits permanently on-chain, and auto-generate SEBI BRSR Core, CDP, and TCFD compliance reports — all from one platform built for India's CCTS mandate and net zero economy.
